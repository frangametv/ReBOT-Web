01 Who runs ReBOT

ReBOT is a Discord bot developed and operated by FranGameTv. This policy describes how data is processed by the bot and its presentation website.

For questions about how your data is used or to exercise your rights, contact the operator through the official Discord support server.

02 What data is used

Open each category to see what information ReBOT processes.

Discord identifiers and settings

User, server, and channel IDs; channels selected for welcome and goodbye messages; and community settings. The bot may process usernames, display names, and permission information to respond to commands and send notifications.

Wallets, banking, and rewards

Virtual currency balances, virtual account identifiers and balances, timestamps of the most recent daily and work rewards, and the community treasury and level. ReBOT accounts are game records, not real bank accounts. Virtual account identifiers provide access to those accounts and should not be shared publicly.

Invites, joins, and departures

Invite codes and usage counts are compared in memory. When possible, the bot stores the inviter ID associated with a member in the database. Join and leave events trigger rewards, balance adjustments, and notifications.

Interactions and operational logs

ReBOT receives slash commands and the parameters needed to execute them. The process may log usernames, user IDs, command names, server information, and join and leave events.

ReBOT does not request the Message Content or Presence intents: it does not monitor ordinary conversations or online status. Information you submit through slash commands is processed to carry out your request.

03 Why this data is needed

  • Display and update balances, manage virtual accounts, and transfer Cash.
  • Apply reward cooldowns and preserve progress after a restart.
  • Identify inviters when possible, award eligible rewards, and adjust balances after departures.
  • Manage community donations, treasury, and levels.
  • Send notifications in configured channels and troubleshoot operational issues.

The Server Members Intent provides the member join and leave events needed for automatic invite features.

04 Where data is stored and for how long

Database and data protection

Persistent data is stored outside Discord in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase). Firestore automatically encrypts data at rest and protects data in transit with TLS.

Retention periods

The current version of the bot does not automatically delete records within 30 days. Balances, settings, and inviter associations may be retained beyond that period. Leaving a server or removing the bot does not automatically delete data.

Hosting, logs, and backups

This project uses Fly.io as the hosting provider for the bot process, but this is subject to changes.

05 Who can see the information

Discord handles bot interactions and messages. Google Cloud/Firebase provides the database, and the hosting provider runs the bot process. The operator may access data to run the service and respond to support requests.

Welcome and goodbye notifications may show the member, the inviter, and the Cash adjustment to anyone who can access the configured channel. Depending on the command, responses may display economy information to the requester or in the channel. Discord log channels, if configured, may contain operational events.

06 Data access and deletion

You can contact the operator to request information about your data, corrections, or deletion. Requests are handled manually: the bot currently has no automated deletion command.

Provide your Discord user ID and, where relevant, the server ID. The operator may request identity verification before taking action. Virtual accounts may require additional private verification because account records do not include an owner ID. Do not post access codes in public channels.

Deletion may result in the loss of balances and progress. The operator still needs to specify handling times, how inviter references are addressed, and when any backup copies expire.

07 Privacy on this website

This is a static website prepared for GitHub Pages. Its code does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, data collection forms, or browser storage. The command demos run locally on the page and do not send data to the bot or its database.

Fonts and the logo are loaded from this website. The “Copy request” feature writes the template to your clipboard only when you press the button.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening links to Discord or Top.gg takes you to external services governed by their own privacy policies.
