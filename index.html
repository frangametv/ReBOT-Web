03 / INVITE REWARDS

Invite friends.
Get rewarded.

When someone joins the server through your invite, ReBOT detects it and rewards eligible joins. If that member leaves, the reward is removed from your balance.

Invite detection depends on the data available from Discord. Returning members with existing records do not trigger another joining reward.
